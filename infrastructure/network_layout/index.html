    L3 Layout / VLANs

    TL;DR: every VLAN is routed by GW, all of them can reach Shared Services, and the only way in from outside is the front door on GW. The old Automation, DMZ, Storage, and AD Lab VLANs were dropped when the router was rebuilt; what they were used for now lives somewhere else, noted below.

    The VLANs, their blocks, and the dynamic DHCP range each one hands out, are one table in the repo (common/vlans.nix); the fixed addresses below are another (common/dns-records.nix). Both are rendered into dnsmasq's config on the router at deploy time, so a device and a name are each one line in one file.

    Mill Network - VLAN '1'

    The mill provides internet access via a gigabit internal network. It can be a bit unreliable but it works for our general usage.

    • ISP: Awareness Software Ltd (AS34931)
    • Mill Network Administrator: Mark Nightingale, Image Village.

    Access to the mill network is provided by a singular CAT5 delivered to the rear of the main space, just above where Rack 1 is.

    • VLAN: 1 (untagged)
    • Subnet: 192.168.20.0/24
    • Gateway: 192.168.20.1
    • DHCP Enabled

    Usage is bound by the LBPT Tenant Network Agreement.

    Phone - VLAN 222

    IP Range: 10.3.22.0/24, no dynamic range - a device arriving on it gets no lease, which is the point of the VLAN existing. The handsets and the intercom are on fixed addresses.

    CS_VLAN - VLAN 1337

    The other network in the building, arriving on the router's LAN trunk with its own tag: 192.168.2.0/24, DHCP 192.168.2.150 - 192.168.2.250. GW routes it but owns none of its addresses.

    IPv6

    AAISP delegate 2001:8b0:1d14::/48, and each VLAN gets a /64 named after it (2001:8b0:1d14:225::/64, ...226::/64, and so on) alongside a ULA in fd99:dead:beef:<vlan>d::/64. Addresses are SLAAC via router advertisements; all IPv6 leaves through the AAISP tunnel, so if that tunnel is down the network has no IPv6 at all.

    Retired

    • Automation - VLAN 229 (10.3.16.0/24): dropped at the 2026-10-21 router cutover. The door system and the automation run from services1 now.
    • DMZ - VLAN 230 (81.187.195.16/29): the AAISP /29 is not a VLAN, it's routed to the tunnel. Nothing is published except through the front door.
    • Storage - VLAN 231: never implemented, and now moot.
    • AD Lab - VLAN 232 (192.168.10.0/24, GOAD): the lab VMs are stopped and the VLAN is not created.

    Out of Band Access

    'Leigh OOB' and its Tailscale-on-a-Pi are gone. Every flake machine, the switches (over the LAN only), and the router are reachable over the tailnet, and the router, the uplinks, the leases and the live connection table are all visible at network-info.int.leighhack.org.
